FAQ

Do my photos get uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser; designs are saved on this device (IndexedDB).

Can I write in Bangla?

Yes. Bengali fonts (Hind Siliguri, Noto Serif Bengali) are built in. Keep letter spacing at 0 for Bengali so letters stay joined.
